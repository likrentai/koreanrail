About the data

  • Source: Korea Railroad Corporation (KORAIL) open data, from data.go.kr, the Korean Railway Statistics portal (railstat.korail.com) and KORAIL's metro rail statistics (info.korail.com).
  • Monthly station figures (2004 to 2022) cover 50 KTX stations; stations with fewer than 500 riders in a month show as 0. Yearly figures (2016 to 2024) cover all KTX and SRT stations.
  • Seoul station's yearly figures include Gangneung line KTX riders; the monthly ones do not, so the two differ by 7 to 10% from 2018.
  • Metro hours are gate taps, all days of 2025 together (weekdays and weekends are not separated). They show the shape of the day, not exact station totals.
